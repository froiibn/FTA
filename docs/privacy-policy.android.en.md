# FTA Android Privacy Policy
Effective: 1 November 2026

FTA (File Transfer Anything) is maintained by SEIZIA. Support and privacy inquiries: https://github.com/froiibn/FTA/issues. Do not post private file contents, passwords, private keys or other confidential information in public issues.

FTA directly transfers the photos, files, text and URLs you select to devices on the same trusted local network. It does not use a developer-operated account service, central transfer server, advertising SDK or analytics SDK. No application content is sent to SEIZIA for advertising, analytics or cloud storage.

Discovery advertises a device ID, device name, operating system and service port on the selected local network. The discovery advertisements (NSD/mDNS TXT metadata) are not encrypted and may be visible to other devices on the same LAN. Discovery does not establish trust. On the first transfer to an unregistered device, only the recipient approves registration. No message body or file data is sent before approval. Registered devices must pass online and certificate checks. Transfer traffic uses TLS directly between the participating devices.

Device registrations, settings and transfer history are stored inside the app. The transfer private key is encrypted with an Android Keystore AES key and kept in backup-excluded app storage. Hardware-backed protection depends on the device. The app excludes its identity, registrations and trust settings from Android backup and device migration. These transfer keys are separate from the developer's Play upload/app-signing keys.

Photos use Android's system photo picker where available, with a document-picker fallback. Files use the system document picker. Only your selected items are copied for transfer. Taking a photo delegates to the system camera app using a temporary output URI grant. FTA does not request broad photo-library access, all-files access, location, microphone or direct camera permission. Temporary selected files are removed after successful transfer or when you clear the selection. Received files are exposed in the Android Files app through FTA's restricted document provider. Files and URLs are not automatically executed.

Android 17 and later require local-network permission. If receiving is enabled and you open the app on a trusted LAN, a connected-device foreground service with an ongoing notification keeps transfers available after switching apps or turning the screen off. You can stop it from the notification, the app setting or Android's running-app controls. Notification display permission is optional. Losing the LAN closes sockets and discovery, with at most 60 seconds to reconnect before the service stops. Reopening the app on an approved LAN can resume receiving. FTA does not bypass force-stop, system stops, battery/OEM restrictions or denied permissions. It does not auto-start after boot or provide an offline queue. Continuous background availability is not guaranteed.

Transfer records may contain device names, file names and received message bodies on your device. Long-press a record to delete it; already received files are retained separately. Received files can be deleted from the Files app. Clearing app data or uninstalling removes internal files, history, settings and keys. Android settings let you revoke permissions.

Opening the PC installation guide requests public version metadata from GitHub Releases to show the current installer addresses. If you choose to open a received URL, share a file to another app or open the GitHub support page, that website's or app's privacy policy applies. FTA does not control the recipient's handling of delivered content. We update this policy when app behavior changes.

Saving received photos to the gallery is optional and requires your explicit action from a completed incoming transfer record. FTA validates supported image content and copies the original bytes into Pictures/FTA through Android MediaStore. It does not automatically publish received photos or request access to the rest of your photo library. Gallery copies are separate from app-private originals; they can remain after FTA is uninstalled and may be synchronized or backed up by your system/photo-provider settings. FTA does not initiate that cloud synchronization. You can delete these public copies in your gallery or Files app.
